Jinendravani

🙏 Jain Devotional App with Chalisa, Aarti, and Bal Bodh Section for Kids (9+)

About the App

Jinendravani is a devotional Jain app designed to spread spiritual peace and moral teachings. It features Jain Chalisas, Aartis, Bhajans, and a Bal Bodh section for children — helping them learn about Jainism through stories and values.

🕉️ Jain Chalisas

Read and listen to daily Jain Chalisas and prayers offline.

📖 Bal Bodh

Interactive stories and teachings designed for children aged 9+.

🎵 Aarti & Bhajan

Access devotional audio content for meditation and peace.

🌙 Offline Support

All content available offline once downloaded — no internet needed.
