Privacy Policy

for Jinendravani – Jain Devotional App (Includes Bal Bodh Section)

Effective Date: November 6, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jinendravani, a Jain devotional and educational application offering Chalisa, Aarti, and a Bal Bodh section for children. We value your trust and protect your personal data in line with Indian IT laws and global privacy standards.

2. Information We Collect

  • User-submitted data: Name, Email Address collected through our Help/Contact Form for support.
  • Optional data: Messages or feedback you provide voluntarily.
  • Offline data: Downloads such as Chalisa or Bal Bodh stories stored locally on your device only.

3. Purpose of Data Collection

Your information is used solely to:

  • Respond to help or support requests
  • Notify you about updates or issue resolutions
  • Improve devotional and educational app features

4. Bal Bodh & Children’s Privacy (Age 9+)

Jinendravani includes a Bal Bodh section with Jain moral stories and teachings designed for children aged 9 and above. This content promotes spiritual learning in a safe environment.

  • The app is rated 9+ and intended for family use under parental guidance.
  • We do not collect personal data from children under 9 years.
  • If any data is mistakenly submitted by a child < 9, it will be deleted upon request.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is disclosed only if required by law.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Help-form submissions are retained only while your query is active. You may request deletion anytime via email below.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect feature or legal changes. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
